How Pursuit scores this role
ATS Readiness evaluates requirement coverage, terminology, structure/parseability, chronology, and knockout risk.
Recruiter Screening emphasizes mandatory criteria, seniority/domain fit, recognizable evidence, and logistics.
Hiring Manager Interest emphasizes comparable problems solved, ownership, scale, outcomes, leadership, and the hiring problem.
Until enough real outcome data exists, recruiter and hiring-manager numbers are alignment estimates — not empirical hiring probabilities.
