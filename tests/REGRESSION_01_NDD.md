# Regression Test 01 — ndd Medical Technologies

## Expected top qualification drivers

1. Digital product strategy, roadmap and lifecycle ownership
2. Relevant healthcare / medical technology domain experience
3. Healthcare interoperability — EHR/EMR, healthcare data exchange, APIs
4. Regulated cross-functional development — R&D, Quality, Regulatory, Clinical Affairs
5. Customer insight, commercialization and go-to-market

## Fail conditions

Pursuit fails this test if it:

- Selects introductory company prose as a qualification
- Returns duplicate versions of product strategy / roadmap
- Misses healthcare interoperability
- Treats generic API experience as direct EHR/EMR interoperability
- Treats life-science experience as automatically equivalent to medical-device/digital-health experience
- Treats Engineering/R&D collaboration as full evidence for Quality/Regulatory/Clinical experience
- Reports high confidence while important criteria are unidentified
- Moves an accomplishment beneath the wrong employer or role

## Expected candidate assessment before gap validation

- Product strategy / roadmap: Strong
- Healthcare technology domain: Adjacent until direct evidence is validated
- Healthcare interoperability: Adjacent or Gap
- Regulated cross-functional development: Partial
- Customer insight / commercialization: Strong or Partial

Only unresolved dimensions should trigger validation questions.

Validated answers must be saved to the Verified Evidence Bank and reused for future JDs.
